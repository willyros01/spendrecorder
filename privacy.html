SpendRecorder Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

SpendRecorder does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your expenses: dates, amounts, currencies, vendors, categories and comments.
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach.
  • Your settings, such as your home currency.

All of this is stored only in SpendRecorder’s private database on your device. If your device’s own backup (such as iCloud Backup) is turned on, it is included in that backup, which is controlled by you and your device maker, not by SpendRecorder.

Exchange rates

When you enter an expense in a currency other than your home currency, SpendRecorder asks a public exchange-rate service for the rate: Frankfurter first, then ExchangeRate-API if Frankfurter cannot answer. The request contains only the two currency codes and, for Frankfurter, the date. As with any internet request, these services can see your device’s IP address; their own privacy policies apply to them.

Exports and sharing

When you export your expenses or share one, SpendRecorder hands the file or text to your device’s share sheet. Where it goes next — a message, an email, your Files app — is your choice, and that app’s privacy policy applies.

Camera and photo library

SpendRecorder asks for access to your camera and photo library only so you can attach a receipt photo. It never reads other photos.

Children

SpendRecorder collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
