SPENDRECORDER — PROJECT NOTES
=============================

What it is
  Public iOS app made from Expense Ledger build v13 (repo willyros01/expense-ledger).
  The Expense Ledger web app and its repo stay untouched; SpendRecorder lives in
  willyros01/spendrecorder. Plan: Claude doc "SpendRecorder — iOS App Plan" (Oct 9, 2026).

Decisions (Oct 9, 2026)
  Name SpendRecorder · bundle ID io.github.willyros01.spendrecorder · Team VXMLKHF72B
  One user, one phone: "Acting as", "Submitted by" and the status pills
  (Pending / Approved / Rejected) removed. No Firebase: SQLite on the phone.
  Currencies like Fairpot: per-expense currency picker (home and recent first),
  home currency setting (default CAD). A new expense always starts in Canadian Dollar.
  Rates keep Expense Ledger's dated lookup, with the source always shown:
    Frankfurter (expense date, then nearest date within 10 days) → ExchangeRate-API
    (labelled as today's rate) → last known rate → 1:1 placeholder. Tap to type your own.
  Editing keeps the saved rate unless the date or currency changes.
  Public App Store release: first-use terms screen, privacy and terms pages,
  contact willyros01@gmail.com. iPad layout widened as in Fairpot.
  Fixes from the v13 review: local date instead of UTC; today's rate never labelled as
  the expense's date; HRK and VEF (no longer exist) removed from the list.

Version history
  v1.0.0 (Oct 9, 2026) — first version.
    Copied Expense Ledger v13 into this repo, then converted per the plan.
    Storage: SQLite, one transaction per change (BEGIN … COMMIT / ROLLBACK); the screens are
    re-read from the database after every commit; a failed save changes nothing and says so.
    Receipt photos inside SQLite (own table, deleted with their expense). Delete keeps the
    6-second Undo, then commits. Imports are all-or-nothing.
    Libraries built in (React, SheetJS); no service worker, no Firebase.
    Tested in a browser: 44 checks passed (terms, add/edit/delete/undo, three rate sources,
    own rate, storage failure, rename, import, home currency, exports, iPad layout).

  v1.0.1 (Oct 9, 2026)
    Category dropdown ends with "+ Add new category…" (works like "+ Add new vendor…").
    The add box inside Manage moved to the top (it was hidden at the bottom of the scroll box).
    Vendors and categories always listed A to Z, ignoring capitals, also right after adding.
    Tested in a browser: 49 checks passed.
