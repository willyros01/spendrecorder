## Description (4000)

SpendRecorder keeps a clear record of what you spend, wherever you spend it.

Enter the amount, pick the vendor and category, snap the receipt if you like, and save. SpendRecorder converts it to your home currency and shows exactly which rate it used and where that rate came from.

• ANY CURRENCY — 100+ currencies. The rate is looked up for the expense's own date and saved with it, so old expenses never change. Paid at a different rate? Type your own.

• RECEIPT PHOTOS — keep the receipt with the expense.

• SUMMARIES — totals by month, year, category and vendor, all in your home currency. Tap any row to see what's inside.

• SEARCH AND HISTORY — find any expense by vendor, category or note.

• EXPORT — Excel or CSV, saved to Files or sent by Mail, Messages or WhatsApp. Import an Excel or CSV file too.

• PRIVATE BY DESIGN — no account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone in a real database.

## App Review information

- Sign-in required: **No**
- Notes for the reviewer:
  > SpendRecorder needs no account. Accept the terms on first launch. On New, enter an amount,
  > choose or add a vendor, tap Add expense. Tap the currency box to choose another currency; the
  > rate line under the amount shows its source and can be tapped to type your own rate. History
  > lists expenses (Edit, Share, Delete with Undo). Summary has totals, the home-currency setting,
  > Export/Import and the terms. The only network use is public exchange-rate lookups.
